Nail Hit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1st November 2026

Last updated: 1st November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Nail Hit for Android (“the game”) handles information. The game is developed and published by [pixbix.studios] (“we”, “us”, “our”).

In short

  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics in the game.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one. Those ads come from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information when they are used, as described below.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information about you.
  • The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

1. Information kept on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device:

  • Game progress: levels and bosses completed, stars, coins, experience and player level, unlocked and selected hammers and nails, reward chests and their timers (which use your device’s clock), and which tutorials you have seen.
  • Settings: music and sound-effect volume, vibration, and the reduced camera shake and reduced visual effects options.

This information stays in the game’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the game does not back it up to any online service. It does not include your name, contacts, location or any other personal details.

Like most apps, the game writes short technical messages (for example, which music track is playing, or that an ad finished) to your device’s system log to help diagnose problems. They contain no personal information, and the game does not send them anywhere.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Nail Hit is free to play. It offers optional rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you tap to watch one, for example for an extra heart, coins, time off an opening chest or an extra nail in a boss fight. You never have to watch an ad to play.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, using Google’s Mobile Ads SDK (a software component from Google that is built into the game). The game starts Google’s advertising service only when you choose to watch an ad. When it is used, Google states that its SDK automatically collects and shares the following information:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • Device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID, the app set ID and other identifiers related to accounts signed in on your device.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and states that it is encrypted in transit. Google’s SDK may also store some information in the game’s storage on your device, such as your consent choices.

Google processes this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google’s data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK

Personalised ads and consent

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent message before ads are requested, and Google uses your information only as you agree there. If ads are not personalised, Google may still use information such as the content of the app and your general location to choose ads. You can also limit ad personalisation at any time using the controls in section 6.

3. How we use information

We do not collect personal information about you, and the game has no servers of its own. Your progress and settings are used only on your device, to run the game and remember where you are.

AdMob provides us with reports about ads, such as how many were shown and what they earned. These reports are combined totals and do not identify you.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, because we do not receive any. The information described in section 2 is collected directly by Google through its SDK when you watch an ad, and is handled under Google’s policies. Google may process it in countries other than your own.

5. How long information is kept

  • On your device: your progress and settings are kept until you delete them or uninstall the game (see section 6).
  • By Google: information collected for advertising is kept according to Google’s own rules: How Google retains data it collects.

6. Your choices and deleting information

  • Don’t watch ads. Ads are optional. If you never tap to watch one, the game does not start Google’s advertising service.
  • Delete or reset your advertising ID. On most Android devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads. The exact menu depends on your device.
  • Manage ad personalisation with Google at My Ad Center.
  • Delete the game’s data. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Settings > Apps > Nail Hit > Storage > Clear storage, deletes your progress and settings, together with anything Google’s SDK stored in the game’s storage, including your consent choices. You would be asked again the next time consent is needed. Deleted progress cannot be recovered.
  • Information held by Google. To access or delete information Google holds, use the tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal information, such as the right to access, correct or delete it, or to object to its use. We do not hold personal information about you, but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request (see section 11).

7. Children

Nail Hit is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

8. Device permissions

  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback. You can turn it off in the game’s Settings.
  • Internet and network access and the advertising ID, used only by Google’s advertising service.
  • Standard permissions added by Google’s libraries: keeping the device awake briefly, running short background tasks, and reading basic, non-sensitive phone information such as the type of network.

The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

9. Security

Your progress and settings are stored in the game’s private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Google states that the information its SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.
